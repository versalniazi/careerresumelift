Your LinkedIn headline appears everywhere — search results, comments, connection requests, InMail previews. Yet most professionals default to just their job title, wasting valuable real estate.

## Why Your Headline Matters More Than You Think

LinkedIn's search algorithm weights headline keywords heavily, meaning a generic headline like "Marketing Manager at ABC Company" does little to help recruiters searching for "digital marketing" or "brand strategy" find you.

## The Formula for a Strong Headline

```
[Role/Title] | [Key Specialty or Value] | [Optional: Industry or Result]
```

## Headline Examples by Career Level

**Entry-Level / Early Career**
- "Marketing Graduate | Content Strategy & Social Media | Seeking Brand Marketing Roles"
- "Software Engineering Student | Full-Stack Development | Java, Python, React"

**Mid-Level Professional**
- "Senior Financial Analyst | FP&A, Forecasting & Cost Reduction | SaaS & Fintech"
- "Registered Nurse, ICU | Critical Care & Patient Advocacy | 8+ Years Experience"

**Career Changer**
- "Former Teacher Turned Instructional Designer | Curriculum Development & E-Learning"

**Executive**
- "VP of Operations | Driving Operational Excellence & P&L Growth | Manufacturing & Supply Chain"
- "CFO | Financial Strategy, M&A & Capital Markets | Scaling High-Growth Companies"

**Job Seekers (Open to Work)**
- "Product Manager | B2B SaaS & Growth Strategy | Open to New Opportunities"

## What to Avoid

- Generic titles with no differentiation ("Experienced Professional")
- Buzzword soup with no substance ("Passionate Ninja Rockstar")
- Headlines with no keywords a recruiter would actually search
